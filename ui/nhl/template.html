Read these as measurements, not projections. Single-season xG-RAPM still over-credits a player for strong linemates (a multi-season, box-score-anchored version corrects this — see the Records page, which is built on the refined talent estimate), and goaltending is volatile in small samples — a high GSAx over 20 games is mostly noise. All skater numbers are 5-on-5 even strength only (special teams excluded), floored at the minutes shown. Nothing here is aged, shrunk, or turned into a team projection — that is what Records does.
